Cast — Shadow Brain Puzzle
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Mavna Studio ("we") operates the mobile application Cast — Shadow Brain Puzzle (package com.mavnastudio.castshadow, the "App"). This policy explains how the App handles data.

Summary

The App requires no account, login or sign-up. We do not ask you for your name, e-mail address, phone number, photos or contacts. Your game progress stays on your device.

Data leaves your device for two purposes, and only to Google: advertising (Google AdMob) and usage analytics that help us understand how the game is played (Google Analytics for Firebase). Neither includes your name, your e-mail address or your precise location. We do not sell your data.

You can also reach this policy from within the App: the "Privacy policy" link on the in-game collection screen (the screen that lists the shadows you have found).

Data stored on your device

Game progress — solved figures, settings and counters — is stored locally using Android's standard storage. We cannot access it. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

Data collected and shared through advertising

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure those advertisements, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following and shares it with Google:

  • Device and other identifiers — the device Advertising ID (a resettable identifier), the app set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers relating to signed-in accounts
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address, at city level. The App does not use GPS or precise location
  • App activity — advertisement views, clicks and similar interactions
  • Diagnostics — information about the performance of the App and of the ads SDK, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage, together with device information (model, operating system version, language)

This data is processed for three purposes:

  1. Advertising or marketing — selecting and delivering advertisements
  2. Analytics — measuring advertising performance
  3. Fraud prevention, security and compliance — detecting invalid activity and meeting legal obligations

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Usage analytics

To improve the game — to see where players get stuck, whether the first minutes are clear, and how long a puzzle takes — we use Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC). On our behalf, it collects:

  • In-game events — steps of the tutorial; a puzzle being started, solved or left; solve times (as ranges such as "20-40 seconds", not exact durations); which figure was solved; opening the collection screen; steps related to the rewarded-ad button and to interstitial ads (pressing the button, watching the ad or declining)
  • Session summaries — session length (as a range); whether a figure was solved in the session; whether the game's sound is off, whether the device volume is at zero, and whether another app is playing audio on the device
  • Player properties — whether you newly installed the App or updated from an earlier version; how many figures you have solved; a summary of the kind described above for your first and your latest session
  • Automatic records — first open, session start, engagement time, app and operating system updates, the App being uninstalled; device model, operating system version, language and app version
  • App instance ID — a random identifier Firebase creates for each installation. It does not contain your name, your e-mail address or your device name, and it changes if you uninstall and reinstall the App
  • Approximate location — derived at city level from the device's masked IP address. Precise location is not used

Not collected: the Advertising ID is not collected for usage analytics. Names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers and any text you type are not collected.

How it is used: only to improve the game. This data is not used for advertising; ads personalisation and Google signals are turned off, and usage analytics is not linked to our advertising account.

How long it is kept: 14 months, after which it is deleted automatically.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with any party other than Google.

Your controls

Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time from your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Analytics identifier. Uninstalling and reinstalling the App renews the app instance ID; earlier usage data is not linked to the new installation.

Consent — EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland. If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before serving personalised advertisements, using Google's User Messaging Platform. In these regions, usage analytics also runs only with this consent. You may withdraw or change your choice at any time from within the App, via the "Ad preferences" entry on the in-game collection screen. Outside these regions this entry is not shown.

United States. Residents of US states with applicable privacy laws may contact mavnastudio@gmail.com to exercise opt-out rights.

Purchases

The App contains no in-app purchases and does not process payment information.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and is rated for ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact mavnastudio@gmail.com and we will act.

Data retention

We do not collect personal details such as your name or e-mail address. Usage analytics data is kept on Google's servers on our behalf for 14 months and then deleted automatically; it is not linked to your identity. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored progress. Data collected through advertising is retained in accordance with Google's policies.

Security

Data in transit is protected with industry-standard encryption (TLS). No method of transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page with a revised date. For material changes, an "updated" mark is shown once next to the "Privacy policy" link inside the App.
