Cast — Shadow Brain Puzzle
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Mavna Studio ("we") operates the mobile application Cast — Shadow Brain Puzzle (package com.mavnastudio.castshadow, the "App"). This policy explains how the App handles data.

Summary

The App requires no account, login or sign-up. We do not ask you for your name, e-mail address, phone number, photos or contacts. Your game progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is collected by our advertising partner, Google, as described below.

Data stored on your device

Game progress — solved figures, settings and counters — is stored locally using Android's standard storage. We cannot access it. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

Data collected and shared through advertising

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure those advertisements, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following and shares it with Google:

  • Device and other identifiers — the device Advertising ID (a resettable identifier), the app set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers relating to signed-in accounts
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address, at city level. The App does not use GPS or precise location
  • App activity — advertisement views, clicks and similar interactions
  • Diagnostics — information about the performance of the App and of the ads SDK, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage, together with device information (model, operating system version, language)

This data is processed for three purposes:

  1. Advertising or marketing — selecting and delivering advertisements
  2. Analytics — measuring advertising performance
  3. Fraud prevention, security and compliance — detecting invalid activity and meeting legal obligations

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with any party other than Google.

Your controls

Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time from your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Consent — EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland. If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before serving personalised advertisements, using Google's User Messaging Platform. In these regions you may withdraw or change your choice at any time from within the App, via the "Privacy & ads" entry in the Shadow Notebook. Outside these regions this entry is not shown.

United States. Residents of US states with applicable privacy laws may contact mavnastudio@gmail.com to exercise opt-out rights.

Purchases

The App contains no in-app purchases and does not process payment information.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and is rated for ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact mavnastudio@gmail.com and we will act.

Data retention

We retain no personal data. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored progress. Data collected by Google is retained in accordance with Google's policies.

Security

Data in transit is protected with industry-standard encryption. No method of transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page with a revised date. Material changes will be announced within the App.
